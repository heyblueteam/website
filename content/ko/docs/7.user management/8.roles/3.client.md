In Blue, "Client Users" are a unique type of user role designed for clients and vendors. This role is ideal for clients or vendors that need to contribute to conversations and provide feedback without the ability to create new projects or delete other members' items but can still invite the same or below role access.

## 역할 및 권한

A Client User in Blue is primarily focused on feedback/review within the project. Their permissions include:

- **멤버 추가**: Client Users can invite other client roles or below (comment or view only)
- **필드 편집**: 기존 레코드의 필드를 업데이트하여 내용을 보강하거나 세부 정보를 수정할 수 있지만, 레코드를 새로 만들거나 삭제할 수는 없습니다.
- **댓글 추가**: They can actively participate in discussions by adding comments to existing records or tasks.
- **읽기 접근**: They can read all the comments and view the details within the project, keeping them informed and engaged.

## 제한 사항

클라이언트 사용자는 토론에 참여하고 필드 값을 편집할 수 있지만, 여러 측면에서 여전히 접근이 제한됩니다.

- **삭제 기능 없음**: They cannot delete tasks.
- **제한된 접근**: 기존 레코드의 필드 편집과 댓글 작성으로 권한이 제한되며, 프로젝트 설정을 변경하거나 레코드를 추가·삭제할 수 없습니다.

## 이상적인 사용 사례

This role is particularly useful in scenarios such as:

- **클라이언트 피드백**: Allowing clients to provide input on specific tasks or projects without giving them full access.
- **외부 협업**: Engaging with external partners or stakeholders who need to comment on ongoing work but do not require full project access.
- **검토 및 승인 프로세스**: Facilitating feedback loops where input from various team members is required, but not full project participation.

## 설정

To set up a Client User in Blue, a Project Administrator needs to:

1. Go to the 'People' tab in the project settings.
2. Add the individual as a user and assign them the 'Client' role.

## 결론

Client Users are essential to Blue’s collaborative environment, allowing for controlled participation in discussions and feedback processes. By integrating these users into your project, you can ensure that all necessary parties are involved in the conversation while maintaining the integrity and security of the project’s data and workflows.
